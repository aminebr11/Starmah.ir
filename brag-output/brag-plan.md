# Brag Plan: ستاره ماه (Starmah)

## What is this app?
A gamified, interest-personalized learning platform for schools: each child picks a "world"
(fire football, blue-thunder football, Minecraft-style blocks, racing) and the *same* lesson is
re-skinned into that world's story, vocabulary and rewards.

## The angle
"One lesson, four worlds." The README's own line — the football lover learns through goals,
the car lover through Grand Prix and nitro, but the content is the same — is the unique claim.
We show it literally: one math question card morphing through all four team skins.
Wrapped in the brand's cosmic night sky + golden moon, ending on the website and Instagram handle.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep navy night sky, a golden shooting star streaks across and leaves the brand tagline:
«هر کودک، ستاره‌ای‌ست — در مسیر کشف بی‌پایان»

## Key moments
- Logo emblem (moon + star + book) lands with a Saturn-like golden ring; «ستاره ماه» in gold.
- «درس خواندن این‌بار بازی است!» → the question card 8 × 7 cycles through 4 team skins
  (تیم شلیک آتشین / صاعقه آبی / جنگجویان کریپر / سوپر اسپید) with real narrative units (گل / نیترو / بلوک).
- Tap the right answer ۵۶ → «آفرین!» + «+۲۰ ستاره» sticker + XP bar «سطحِ بعدی نزدیک است!».
- Six feature tiles from the site arrive (آزمون و بازی، امتیاز و ستاره، مأموریتِ روزانه، آزمونِ هوشمند، مطالبِ درسی، ارتباط با والدین).

## Outro / punchline
«آماده‌ای ستاره‌ی کلاس شوی؟» → logo + two pills: 🌐 starmah.ir and Instagram @starmah.ir with a tapped «دنبال کنید» button.

## User flow worth showing
Choose world → answer a themed question → earn stars and level up.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (with app-store polish)
- Creative direction: "a magical night-sky storybook trailer for kids, with premium game-UI moments"
- Interpretation: big golden type, deep space depth, smooth camera drift; UI moments are crisp and tactile.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920 (Instagram Reels / Stories)
## Duration: 24s

## Share copy (draft)

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a bright, playful bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM, beats every ~0.5s from 3.02s)
- Treatment: 0.6s fade-in, ~0.34 volume, 1.2s fade-out at the end
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues 16.02, 20.02, 23.02 — lock the features→outro reveal to 20.02.
  Beat grid used for the 4 skin swaps (every 2 beats ≈ 1.0s, readable) and feature tiles.
- Audio-reactive: subtle — music RMS breathes the moon halo / aurora glow. No waveforms.
- SFX: moderate & warm: whoosh-like soft impact on shooting star, bell on logo, card sounds for skin swaps,
  click on answer tap, glass sparkle on +20 stars, bell on outro, click on follow.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — Hook — 0–3.5s
Star field, aurora, shooting star; tagline in two lines, word-by-word. Hold ≥2s.
Transition: dramatic zoom into the sky → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 3.5–7.5s
Emblem scales in with orbit ring + halo, «ستاره ماه» gold gradient, eyebrow «پلتفرم آموزش هوشمند مدارس · با هوش مصنوعی».
Audio: bell at reveal. Transition: slide up → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — One lesson, four worlds — 7.5–13s
Headline «درس خواندن این‌بار بازی است!», question card cycles 4 skins (~1.05s each, on every other beat).
Caption «یک درس، چهار دنیا». Card sound each swap.

### Scene 4 — Play & reward — 13–16.5s
Three options; tap ۵۶ → green, star burst, «آفرین!» + «+۲۰ ستاره», XP bar fills.

### Scene 5 — Everything a class needs — 16.5–20s
«هر چیزی که کلاس نیاز دارد» + 6 tiles in 3 pairs; full set holds ≥1.2s.

### Scene 6 — Outro — 20–24s
«آماده‌ای ستاره‌ی کلاس شوی؟», logo, website pill, Instagram pill + follow tap. Hold to end.

**Music mood:** upbeat-cinematic. **Audio summary:** a bright bed rising under a starry intro, warm hits on each reveal, ringing out on the handle.
